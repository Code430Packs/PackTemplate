# Licence

Pick the licence that fits this pack and replace this file. Set the same SPDX id in each
`pack.json` `license`.

- **Location packs** made from OpenStreetMap are a derived database of OSM, so they are
  licensed under the **Open Database License 1.0** (`ODbL-1.0`,
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Keep `© OpenStreetMap contributors` in
  `attribution` (the game shows it under the minimap), plus any elevation source's credit.
  Hand fixes and overrides in the repository are part of the database and share its licence.
- **Story, ads and character packs** are your own writing and art. A Creative Commons licence
  such as **CC BY 4.0** (`CC-BY-4.0`) lets others build on them; any licence you choose is fine
  as long as it is stated here and in `pack.json`.
